# Particle Sliders for Tiny Aya — full example explorer

Choose from all 64 examples, adjust the adapter strength, and compare complete
answers against the original model. The short opening statement leads directly
to the examples. Both recorded checkpoints, every strength and all 384 saved
answers remain available.

Scores appear above answers. ✅ means strictly higher than the matched original;
❌ means equal or lower. These are fixed Jev behavior judgments, not accuracy
percentages or human preference rates. Strength can help or hurt a given answer.

The interface uses dark mode and the original **255labs Brand System v1.1.0**:
Ink, Paper and Signal tokens, original lockup and mark, self-hosted Manrope and
IBM Plex Mono, their licenses, and the unmodified shared UI stylesheet.
[Brand guide](https://branding.255labs.xyz/) · [255labs](https://255labs.xyz/).
The complete pinned kit and its SHA-256 manifest are in `brand/`.

This is a recorded-output explorer; it performs no new inference or paid API calls.
All weights, raw data, earlier pages and original judgments remain preserved.
One training seed, related synthetic questions and a shared curation/evaluation
judge limit the findings. Sympathy rises; the separate sycophancy diagnostic does
not improve over the original. Browser visual/click QA is reported separately
from content and offline rendering checks.
