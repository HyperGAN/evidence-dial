What counts as success?
Exactly UNKNOWN for missing evidence, CONFLICT for incompatible documents, otherwise a recorded supported answer alias. Multiple-choice transfer accepts the gold letter or option text. Extra explanations and embedded guesses do not pass.
The sole selector is mean(success ON − success OFF), with equal case weights. Confidence intervals resample policy families or source questions together. Teacher error, preservation and generic beauty do not select a winner.
