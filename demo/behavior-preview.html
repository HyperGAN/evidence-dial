How the behavior is learned

01 · CURATEFind successful contexts

Explore 16 behavioral instructions per training question. Jev judges the replies; CuratorKIT validates and deduplicates the data.

02 · TRAINLearn from internal states

ParticleGAN trains a routed adapter toward the frozen model’s first-answer hidden states under selected instructions. The backbone stays frozen.

03 · EVALUATEKeep the question unchanged

AuditKIT compares OFF, prompt-only, and adapter responses. Checkpoints and strengths are chosen on development data before held-out evaluation.

Checkpoint, evaluation scope, and limitations

Targets are captured before the first response token, rather than from the entire approved response trajectory. New preferences require new curation and training. This study uses a small synthetic corpus, one training seed, the same Jev judge for curation and evaluation, and a 64-token evaluation limit.
